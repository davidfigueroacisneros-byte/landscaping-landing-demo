The 3-Layer Build

Pavers don't fail because of the stone — they fail because of what's under it. Every job gets the same three layers: Base, Drainage, Lock.

Layer 01 · Base

Excavated & compacted foundation

We excavate to engineered depth, compact the native soil, build a crushed-gravel base compacted in passes, and screed a level bed of sand — so every paver sits on structure, not dirt.

Why it matters — a thin or skipped base is why patios sink and wave within a year. It's the layer you never see, and the one that decides everything.

Layer 02 · Drainage

Graded so water walks away

Every surface is graded a consistent 2% away from the structure. Rain sheets off the far edge — it never ponds against your foundation or sits in the joints.

Why it matters — standing water is what erodes bedding sand, stains stone, and heaves surfaces. Drainage is designed in, not hoped for.

Layer 03 · Lock

Edged, sanded & locked tight

Concrete edge restraint pins the field, polymeric sand locks every joint, and a final compaction pass seats the surface. Nothing shifts, spreads, or grows weeds.

Why it matters — an unlocked edge is how tight patios slowly spread into loose ones. The lock is what keeps year 10 looking like day 1.
